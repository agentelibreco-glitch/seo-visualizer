# AI Agentic Services Blueprint & Offer Strategy
**Tailored for Web Development, Digital Marketing & Tech Agencies**

---

## Executive Summary

Transitioning from traditional Web Development & Digital Marketing into an **AI Automation & Agentic Services Agency** allows you to move from one-off projects to high-margin recurring retainers. By leveraging your background in web development, API integrations, and lead generation, you can offer end-to-end intelligent automation that solves critical operational bottlenecks for your clients.

---

## Service Catalog (Prioritized Roadmap)

### 1. Workflow & Process Automation Agents (Primary Offering)
*Automating multi-step operational tasks, content pipelines, lead routing, and business operations.*

* **Key Deliverables:**
  * **Lead Intake & Qualification Agents:** Automatically enrich inbound leads (Clearbit/Apollo), qualify them using LLMs, and schedule calls via Calendar APIs.
  * **Content & SEO Production Pipelines:** Automated research, draft generation, image creation, brand compliance checks, and auto-publishing (WordPress/Webflow/Socials).
  * **ERP/CRM Sync & Data Extraction:** Extract unstructured data from emails/PDF invoices and auto-populate CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive).
* **Tech Stack:**
  * **Orchestration:** n8n (Self-hosted or Cloud), Make.com, Zapier Central.
  * **Code/Frameworks:** Python, LangChain, LangGraph, AutoGen, FastEngine APIs.
  * **AI Models:** Gemini 1.5 Pro/Flash, OpenAI GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet.

### 2. Autonomous Customer Support & Conversational Agents
*24/7 intelligent multi-channel agents capable of taking actions on behalf of users.*

* **Key Deliverables:**
  * **Omnichannel Web & Mobile Chatbots:** Real-time conversational AI trained on client websites, FAQs, and product catalogs.
  * **WhatsApp & SMS Business Agents:** Automated appointment booking, order updates, and customer re-engagement over messaging channels.
  * **AI Voice Agents:** Inbound/Outbound voice assistants for call handling, lead screening, and triage.
* **Tech Stack:**
  * **Voice AI:** Retell AI, Vapi, Bland AI, ElevenLabs.
  * **Messaging Integrations:** Twilio, Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, ManyChat AI.
  * **Frontend Widget:** Custom React/JS widgets integrated directly into client websites (Shopify, Webflow, custom apps).

### 3. Custom Knowledge Base & Enterprise RAG Systems
*Connecting LLMs securely to proprietary client datasets for internal search and intelligence.*

* **Key Deliverables:**
  * **Internal Team Copilots:** Chat with internal company SOPs, HR docs, technical documentation, and Notion/Google Drive databases.
  * **Document Parsing & Extraction:** Intelligent processing of contracts, receipts, and technical specs into structured JSON/database records.
  * **Data Analytics & Reporting Agents:** SQL generation and automated weekly executive dashboards.
* **Tech Stack:**
  * **Vector Databases:** Pinecone, Qdrant, ChromaDB, pgvector (PostgreSQL).
  * **Embeddings & LLMs:** Gemini Text Embeddings, OpenAI Embeddings, Gemini 1.5 Pro (long-context processing up to 2M tokens).
  * **Frameworks:** LlamaIndex, LangChain.
